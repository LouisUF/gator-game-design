▶ LEVEL 1 · WEEKLY MEETINGS

TUESDAYS
@ 6PM

At our weekly meetings, we will:

  • Learn about the design behind your favorite games
  • Brainstorm ideas for our own board/card/video games
  • Discuss interesting modern design questions
  • Explore how to design new mechanics for games - and how to break them

In addition to weekly meetings, we will also have various events throughout the year like game nights, design-a-thons, and other fun activities based on your suggestions!

▶ LEVEL 2 · DESIGN QUESTIONS

WHAT IF...?

Some questions we would discuss at a meeting:

"What would happen if you added a fourth option to rock paper scissors?"

"How could you turn Minecraft into a horror game?"

"Why do my parents like Candy Crush so much?"

▶ LEVEL 3 · DESIGN vs DEVELOPMENT

DESIGN vs
DEVELOPMENT

We're a design-focused club — no technical background required. Just bring yourself and your unique brand of creativity and curiosity.

Rather than coding games in Unity or Unreal, we write game design documents.

Rather than asking HOW to program a card game, we ask WHY card games are fun.

Rather than debugging code, we challenge the design foundations behind games.
